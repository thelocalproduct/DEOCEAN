Active Breeding Experiment Details

Our premier Japanese Koi (Cyprinus rubrofuscus) breeding program focuses on maintaining pristine color clarity, thick jumbo frames, and immaculate shiroji (snow-white skin) paired with deep lacquer-red hi.

Maintained under strict mud-pond maturation cycles, natural seasonal temperature variations, and selective culling protocols supervised by master breeders to ensure supreme conformation and longevity.

  • Lineage Code: KOI-X1 Dainichi & Sakai Bloodline Mix
  • Target Trait: Deep Hi Conformation & Flawless Snow Shiroji
  • Generation Stage: F4 Certified Grand Champion Lineage
  • Optimal Water Parameters: pH 7.2 - 7.8 | 18°C - 24°C
